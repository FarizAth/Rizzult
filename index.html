The System

Find. Reach. Qualify. Grow.

The full outbound engine runs in seven stages. Grouped into four, here's what actually happens.

STEPS 01–02

ICP & Account Targeting

We define your ideal customer profile and find the companies that actually match it, not just ones that look similar.

STEP 03

Decision-Makers & Signals

We identify the right people inside each account and layer in buying signals that point to real timing.

STEPS 04–05

Personalized Outreach

Messaging is built around the prospect, the account, and the signal, drafted by AI and reviewed by a person before it sends.

STEPS 06–07

Qualification & Pipeline

Responses are reviewed for real buying interest before anything lands on your calendar.

Fit

Built For B2B Companies With Something Real To Sell.

Rizzult isn't for every business. It's built for a specific kind of company.

Good Fit

  • A clear offer and an identifiable ideal customer profile
  • Products or services with meaningful deal values
  • Sales capacity to handle new qualified conversations
  • Currently too dependent on referrals for growth
  • Wants a more predictable pipeline, not a one-off list

Not A Fit

  • No clearly defined ideal customer yet
  • Consumer or very low-ticket products
  • No one available to take new sales conversations
  • Looking for the cheapest possible list of emails
